Drawing Canvas

Instructions:

  • Select Tool: Click on shapes to select them (highlighted in red)
  • Drag Shapes: When a shape is selected, drag it to move
  • Rectangle/Square: Click and drag to draw
  • Circle: Click for center, drag to set radius
  • Triangle: Click and drag to define size
  • Line: Click and drag to draw a line
  • Delete: Remove the currently selected shape
  • Clear All: Remove all shapes from canvas
